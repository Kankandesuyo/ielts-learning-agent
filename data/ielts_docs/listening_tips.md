# IELTS Listening Tips

Listen for correction signals such as "not", "actually", "sorry", and "I mean".
The first number or place you hear may be a trap.

Common paraphrases include quarter past nine for 9:15, part-time work for a job
while studying, and main entrance for front door.

For map questions, track direction words carefully: opposite, next to, behind,
on the left, and past the reception desk.
