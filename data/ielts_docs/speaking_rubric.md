# IELTS Speaking Rubric Notes

This MVP is a text practice coach, not an official examiner.

Fluency and Coherence means the answer is easy to follow, has enough detail, and
does not rely on memorized template phrases.

Lexical Resource means the student can use natural topic words and paraphrase
basic ideas.

Grammatical Range and Accuracy means the student can speak with mostly accurate
sentences and some controlled complex structures.

Pronunciation cannot be judged from typed text. A real audio module should be
added later.
