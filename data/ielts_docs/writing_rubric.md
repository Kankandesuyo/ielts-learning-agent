# IELTS Writing Rubric Notes

This is not the official rubric text. It is a learning summary for the MVP.

Task Response or Task Achievement checks whether the answer addresses the task,
develops a clear position, and supports ideas with relevant examples.

Coherence and Cohesion checks paragraph logic, progression of ideas, and whether
linking words show real relationships.

Lexical Resource checks topic vocabulary, collocation, repetition, and word-form
accuracy.

Grammatical Range and Accuracy checks sentence control, tense, articles, subject
verb agreement, punctuation, and the accuracy of complex structures.
