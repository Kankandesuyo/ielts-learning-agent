# IELTS Reading Tips

Before answering, locate names, dates, numbers, and noun phrases. IELTS questions
often replace passage words with synonyms.

True means the statement agrees with the passage. False means the statement
contradicts the passage. Not Given means the passage does not provide enough
information.

For Matching Headings, read the first and last sentence of each paragraph and
choose the heading that captures the whole paragraph, not one detail.
